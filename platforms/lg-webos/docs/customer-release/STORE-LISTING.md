# LG listing copy — draft

App name: Lounge TV

Short description:
Your Lounge TV membership, on the big screen.

Description:
Watch the Live TV, movies and series included with your Lounge TV membership through a TV-friendly interface.

Browse organised categories, check available programme listings, save favourites and pick up supported titles where you left off. Sport Lounge brings upcoming fixtures together with event information and channel discovery.

Sign in using your Lounge TV username and password, or scan the QR code and enter your details on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi as your TV.

An active Lounge TV account and internet connection are required. Available content, programme information, audio and subtitles depend on your membership and the selected stream.

Category recommendation: Entertainment.
Languages implemented: English.
Initial markets recommendation: New Zealand and Australia, subject to the final Seller Lounge selection.
