# LG review UX scenario — draft

App: Lounge TV. Package version 1.0.0. App ID nz.loungetv.webos.

1. Launch Lounge TV from LG Home. Native launch art leads to the branded in-app splash.
2. First launch shows QR phone sign-in and remote-control username/password entry. QR requires the phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi. Enter the supplied review account credentials; no server address is required.
3. After successful sign-in the app prepares Live TV, Movies, Series and the programme guide before entering Home. Invalid/inactive credentials return a recoverable sign-in message.
4. Home features event banners and available catalogue rails. Directional navigation and pointer selection open an item or section. Team fixtures display team crests; individual events use titles and background artwork.
5. Live TV uses category/subcategory navigation and a channel list. Selecting a channel starts native/adaptive playback as appropriate. From fullscreen, Left opens the channel panel and Left again opens category selection. Back unwinds panels and playback.
6. Guide scrolls through channel/programme rows. Programme information depends on the published source; some continuous/event streams have no timed schedule.
7. Movies and Series open their catalogue with All selected. Select a title, inspect details and play. Resume, seeking and episode selection are available where supported.
8. Saved holds favourites and event reminders. Settings exposes supported LG controls, playlists/profiles, guide refresh and diagnostics.
9. Pressing Back on entry Home/Login returns to LG Home. Nested views and dialogs unwind before exiting.
10. Reopen to verify remembered login. Sign out in Settings/playlist management when finished.

Review account: TO BE PROVIDED SECURELY IN SELLER LOUNGE. Use a dedicated active review account with appropriate duration, not Gary's personal or a customer's account. No credentials appear in this document or package.

The flow is implemented; all physical results must be recorded on PHYSICAL-ACCEPTANCE.md and the official LG self-checklist before submission.
