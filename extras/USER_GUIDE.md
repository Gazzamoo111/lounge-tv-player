# Lounge TV | Customer Guide

**For Fire TV, Android TV and Google TV**  
**Updated:** 25 October 2026 · **Guide edition:** 1.0

Welcome to Lounge TV. This guide covers installing the Lounge TV player, signing in, finding your channels, watching movies and series, making favourites, and getting help.

**Need help now?** Visit **https://loungetv.nz/setup-guide** on your phone. For account activation, renewals or a channel issue, reply to the Lounge TV support contact that sent your subscription details. Please don't send login passwords in public comments.

> **Important:** Lounge TV is the name of both a subscription service and the Android TV player. This guide is for the **Lounge TV player on compatible Android-based television devices**. Smart TV and Apple/iOS devices use different players; follow the device-specific instructions Lounge TV sends you. Available programmes and channels depend on your active subscription and the current programme feeds.

---

## 1. Start here — new customer setup

### What you need
- A supported **Android TV, Google TV or compatible Fire TV** device.
- A steady internet connection.
- Your **Lounge TV activation message**, including the server address, username and password where applicable.
- The TV remote. A phone on the same Wi-Fi is useful if you prefer QR-assisted entry.

If you've requested a trial, use the activation details **after Lounge TV confirms the trial is active**. Trial and subscription logins aren't created by installing the app.

### Install Lounge TV on a Fire TV Stick

1. On the Fire TV home screen, search for **Downloader** and install it.
2. If asked, allow Downloader to install apps from unknown sources in the Fire TV device settings. Menu names depend on Fire OS version.
3. Open Downloader and enter the Lounge TV download address from **https://loungetv.nz/setup-guide** or from your activation message.
4. Download the Lounge TV APK, choose **Install**, then **Open**.
5. If installation is blocked, check the unknown-app installation setting and available storage. **Do not factory-reset the TV**.
6. After installation, you can remove the downloaded installer APK from Downloader to free storage. Do not delete the installed Lounge TV app.

Not every device sold as a “Fire TV Stick” has Android-based Fire OS. Some newer models use a different operating system and cannot install Android APKs. Check your device model before using Downloader; ask Lounge TV if you're unsure.

### Install on Android TV or Google TV

1. Open the device's browser or supported downloader app.
2. Use the download instructions provided on **https://loungetv.nz/setup-guide**.
3. Approve the installation if the TV asks, then launch **Lounge TV**.
4. Choose the profile and login setup below.

**Samsung, LG, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV:** the Lounge TV Android APK is not the correct installer for these devices. Request the relevant player instructions rather than trying to install an APK.

### First launch

The app's first-run wizard goes through **Welcome → Display → Before you start → Set up Lounge TV → Profile → Add a source → Import**.

1. Select your language, screen size and text display preferences.
2. Select **New profile**. Give the profile a name; you can also set an avatar or PIN.
3. Choose to add a playlist. You can use **From phone / Remote** or **Type it here**.
4. For a normal Lounge TV subscription, select **Xtream** and enter the server address, username and password supplied in your activation message.
5. Select **Start import**. Keep the app open while the first list loads, or use the offered continue-in-background option.
6. Wait for channels, movies, series and guide data to populate. Large catalogues can take a few minutes. The programme guide may continue filling in after the main list appears.

The setup wizard also offers restore and transfer options for existing installations. New customers usually don't need those.

### Enter details by phone instead of the remote

1. On the TV's Add a source screen, choose **From phone / Remote**.
2. The TV displays a QR code, local address and a temporary PIN.
3. Put your phone on the **same Wi-Fi network** as the TV.
4. Scan the TV's QR code (or enter the displayed address), then enter the TV's PIN when prompted.
5. Complete the source form on your phone and submit it.
6. **Return to the TV**. Check the pre-filled source details and select **Start import**.

**The QR transfer enters details on your local TV.** It does not create a subscription or activate a trial. If scanning fails, confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi and try the manual entry option.

### Returning customer / existing device

If your Lounge TV app is already installed with your account configured, **do not uninstall it** just to update the channels or app. Use the refresh or update procedures in sections 7 and 8. Uninstalling or clearing application data can remove saved playlists, history, profiles and favourites.

## 2. The remote — essentials

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Up / Down / Left / Right** | Move focus between cards, menus and controls. |
| **OK / Select** | Open a screen, select a programme or start playback. |
| **Back** | Return to the previous screen; while watching, return to browsing. |
| **Press and hold OK** | Open extra options on supported channels, movies, series or guide items. |
| **Menu** | Opens channel options on supported remotes/screens. |

On cards, a short press generally opens or plays. A long press is where you find actions such as favourites or hide, when that card supports them. Remote keys differ by device.

## 3. Getting around Lounge TV

The main navigation area takes you to **Home, Live TV, TV Guide, Movies, Series, Search and More**. Sports and Downloads may also be available through the navigation or Home shortcuts, depending on your layout.

### Home

Home is your starting point, with a featured title area and rows chosen for your profile.

- **Featured hero:** browse highlighted titles. **Play** opens the current title. **Trailer**, when offered, tries a YouTube preview; a blocked or unavailable embedded trailer may offer **Open in YouTube** instead.
- **Keep Watching:** resume a film or episode you've started.
- **Live TV Highlights:** shortcuts into available live channels.
- **Featured Movies / Popular Series:** browse titles with artwork.
- **Live Sports:** shortcuts to Sports Hub, Rugby, UFC & Boxing, Cricket and Sports Channels; match cards appear when suitable fixtures are available.
- **Favourites:** return quickly to channels, movies or series you've saved.

Some rows only appear when relevant content exists. You can adjust certain rows through **More → Settings → Home**.

### My Lounge and favourites

Use the **star** icon to save your favourites. The favourites area is your shortcut to frequently watched channels and titles; on screens that call it **My Lounge**, it is the same personalised idea.

To favourite an item, focus the channel or title, **hold OK** to open options and choose the **star / Favourite** action. You can also find favourites in **More → Favourites**.

### Search

Open **Search**, enter a title or channel name, and choose the matching result. Results depend on the content your account provides.

## 4. Live TV — watch and organise channels

### Find a channel

1. Open **Live TV** from the menu.
2. Use the channel list or browse its categories.
3. Focus a channel to see its information or preview where available.
4. Press **OK** to play or enter full-screen viewing.

**A channel in a list is not a guarantee that its stream is currently working.** If a channel repeatedly fails, report it to Lounge TV with the channel name and what happens.

### Favourite or hide channels

- Highlight a channel and **hold OK** (or use the Menu key where supported).
- Choose **Add to favourites**, **Hide** or another available action.
- Review hidden entries using the relevant organise/restore option in the app.

**Hiding isn't deleting.** These are personal display preferences. If you can't find a channel, first check your category, search and hidden items.

### The channel picture is frozen, black or wrong

1. Try the channel once more, then test a **different channel**.
2. Check whether other streaming apps on the same TV have internet.
3. If only one channel fails, send Lounge TV its **exact name and category**.
4. If several channels in the **same category** fail, report the whole category. Do not repeatedly reinstall the app.
5. If all channels fail, check your subscription details and whether another device is already using the connection allowance.

Lounge TV can investigate the feed with the streaming provider; a player update alone does not repair a faulty provider stream.

## 5. TV Guide (EPG)

Open **TV Guide** from the menu, or switch between list and guide views in Live TV.

- Browse what is on now and what's coming next.
- Select a programme to see its details or open the channel.
- Use the available reminder option for a future programme.
- If the schedule is blank, the first EPG sync may still be in progress.

### Guide missing or showing the wrong time

1. Check the TV's **date, time and timezone** are correct.
2. Open **More → Settings → Sources & guide → EPG Sources**.
3. Refresh available guide data, or ask Lounge TV if the login has no EPG attached.
4. If only some programmes don't match their channels, try **Auto-match EPG** or request help.

If the entire schedule is off by a fixed number of hours, check the guide time-offset setting before manually changing lots of channel matches.

## 6. Movies and Series

### Movies
Open **Movies**, choose a category and press **OK**. At the top of each category, **Featured Movies** and **New Releases** highlight available films in larger cards. Press **Left / Right** to browse up to 20 films in each row; **Down** continues into the full movie catalogue. The Featured selection changes periodically, and New Releases highlights films by their release year. If a category has no suitable new titles, that row may be absent.

Use a title's details screen to play it, resume it or review its information where available.

**Keep your catalogue tidy — without deleting films or channels**

1. Open **More → Settings → Customise** (sometimes labelled **Customize** or **Content**, depending on the settings view).
2. Open the relevant content section: **Movies**, **Series** or **Live TV**.
3. Hide unwanted categories or individual titles (for example, duplicate channels, foreign-language films or categories you never use).
4. Return to Movies or Live TV: hidden entries should disappear from the browse lists and category highlight rows.
5. To bring content back, return to **Settings → Customise** and restore or unhide the affected category or item.

Hiding content changes what you see in your profile; it does **not** cancel your service or erase movies from your provider's catalogue. If a title disappears unexpectedly, check hidden categories and hidden items before resetting your source. Avoid deleting the playlist or app data to clean up the catalogue.

**Browsing tip:** When choosing a new Movies category, the list opens at the **top**, starting with Featured or New Releases. From the first card of either highlight row, press **Left** to return to the category menu.

### Series
Open **Series**, choose a category and browse **Featured Series** and **New Series** at the top. Like Movies, these are horizontal highlights with a large cinematic hero above them; scrolling down reveals the complete series catalogue. The hero updates to describe the focused show. Featured selections change periodically, while New Series uses the show's original release year, not the date the provider added it.

Press **Left** from the first featured show to return to Categories. Selecting a different category returns to its top highlights. Press **OK** on a show to open its seasons and episodes. When available, **Continue Watching** resumes from your saved position. Whether the next episode starts automatically depends on your playback setting.

If a series or category is unwanted, use **More → Settings → Customise** to hide it. Hidden shows should not appear in the Featured or New Series rows; you can restore them through the same Settings area.

### Trailers
A **Trailer** button appears only when a title has a trailer reference. Lounge TV tries the embedded YouTube player. If YouTube blocks the embed or your device's WebView cannot play it, the app can offer to open YouTube externally. Trailers are a separate feature from playing your subscription's movies and episodes.

### Favourites and history
Hold OK on supported titles to add favourites, and use **More → History** to review what you've watched. Progress and history are tied to your current profile.

## 7. Sports

Open **Sports** or use the **Live Sports** row near the bottom of Home.

- **Sports Hub** shows fixtures, live results and upcoming games for the sports or leagues you follow.
- Select a fixture to open its match page.
- Where a matching channel is found in your TV Guide, the match page can suggest it.
- Use the Sports settings to choose sports, leagues or teams.

**Important:** Sports fixtures and scores are information services. They do not by themselves unlock a stream or guarantee a broadcaster for a match. Available sports channels are determined by your active subscription.

If Sports shows no fixtures, check its followed sports/leagues and try refreshing. If a listed match has no broadcast option, browse Sports Channels or Live TV.

## 8. Refreshing the channel list

**Refresh the playlist — don't reinstall the app.**

1. Open **More → Settings**.
2. Open **Sources & guide → Playlists** (or **Manage Sources**, depending on your selected settings view).
3. Select your Lounge TV source.
4. Choose the available **Refresh / Re-sync** action.
5. Allow time for the updated categories, channels, movies and series to import.

A normal re-sync updates content from the configured source. Only use a destructive or clean re-import if Lounge TV support specifically asks you to; customisations can be affected.

If a source reports **incorrect login** or **expired subscription**, re-syncing alone won't reactivate it. Contact Lounge TV using your activation message.

## 9. Updating the Lounge TV app

The app update is **different** from refreshing channels.

### Update from inside Lounge TV
1. Open **More → About** or **More → Settings → App**.
2. Choose **Check for updates**.
3. If a newer **Lounge TV** version is offered, select the update and follow the TV installer prompts.
4. Reopen Lounge TV and check that your profile and playlist are still there.

Only the regular, signed production Lounge TV app can install production updates over itself. Test builds use a separate app identity and are updated separately.

### If the update fails
- Make sure the TV has enough free storage and a stable connection.
- If the TV blocks the installer, review the device's installation permissions.
- If the error mentions a conflicting package or signature, **do not uninstall the production app**. Contact Lounge TV with the exact error.
- You can also use the current installation directions at **https://loungetv.nz/setup-guide**.

**Never choose Clear Data or Uninstall as the first update troubleshooting step.** Those actions can remove your login and personalised settings.

## 10. Profiles, display and accessibility

### Profiles
Go to the current profile selector or **More → Settings → Profiles** to edit your profile. Profiles can have different favourites, viewing history and settings. A Kids profile and profile PIN are available.

### Appearance
Within Settings, you can adjust options such as text size, interface zoom, themes, colours and the launcher icon. Restarting the app or launcher may be necessary for an icon change to appear on the TV home screen.

### Subtitles and sound
While playing a title, use the available player controls for audio tracks, subtitles and sync. If there is no alternative track listed, the feed may not provide one.

### Playback reliability
Use your device's normal playback settings unless a particular stream has trouble. If sound or video fails on one channel only, report the channel instead of changing global settings.

## 12. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | First thing to try |
| --- | --- |
| **No channels after signing in** | Give the import time, check the current profile, then re-sync the source. |
| **Login rejected** | Check the server URL, username and password against your Lounge TV activation message; check subscription status. |
| **Whole NZ category is broken or wrong** | Tell Lounge TV support the category and examples; don't add random playlists or reinstall. |
| **One channel buffers** | Try other channels; report the exact failing channel if the others work. |
| **Every channel buffers** | Restart the TV/network equipment, check Wi-Fi strength and other devices sharing the connection. |
| **Blank guide** | Refresh the EPG after the initial import and check the TV clock. |
| **Can't find a favourite** | Search for it, inspect favourites and check whether it was hidden. |
| **Film won't resume** | Check that you're using the same profile; try opening it again through Keep Watching. |
| **Trailer does not play** | Use the offered external YouTube option, if available; not all videos permit embedded playback. |
| **App update fails** | Don't uninstall; note the exact error and contact Lounge TV. |
| **App crashes or freezes** | Force-stop and reopen it; restart the device if necessary. Avoid clearing app data. |

### What to send when requesting help

To help us diagnose the issue quickly, include:
- **Device:** Fire TV Stick model / Android TV / Google TV model.
- **Problem:** exactly what happens.
- **Where:** Home, Live TV, NZ channels, Movies, Series, Sports or Settings.
- **Example:** channel name or title, and approximate time of the failure.
- **Screenshot or short clip**, if possible.

Never publish your subscription password or full login details.

## 13. Subscription, renewals and support

Lounge TV sends trial and paid activation details through its customer communication channels. **A 24-hour trial, where offered, starts when your account is activated**, not when you first open the player.

Subscriptions are handled separately from the player app. Renewals are **not automatically charged by the Lounge TV player**. If your access expires or you'd like to renew, use the official payment instructions sent by Lounge TV.

- **Customer setup:** https://loungetv.nz/setup-guide
- **Lounge TV website:** https://loungetv.nz
- **Account, billing, channel and technical support:** use the verified Lounge TV support contact supplied with your activation message.

**Please do not contact the developer of the original open-source player for your Lounge TV account or channel service.** Lounge TV is your service contact.
